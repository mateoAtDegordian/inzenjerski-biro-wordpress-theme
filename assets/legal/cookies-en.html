Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files which are stored on your device (computer, mobile telephone or tablet) when you visit the ingbiro.hr website.

We have designed the website ingbiro.hr in such a way as to respect your privacy to the greatest possible extent. Our website uses solely strictly necessary first-party cookies, which are indispensable for the proper functioning of the website, i.e., we do not use any third-party analytical, advertising or marketing cookies (such as Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel and similar).

Data collected by means of strictly necessary cookies - Our website uses solely cookies which are technically indispensable for its proper and secure functioning.

Data which we process: visitor session data (session identifier, protection of forms against attacks by unauthorised persons).

Purpose of processing: proper display of the website, technical stability and security (prevention of misuse of registration forms).

Legal basis for processing: the legitimate interest of the Data Controller in maintaining a secure and technically stable website (Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) – Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council).

Consent

In accordance with Article 100(4) of the Electronic Communications Act, the storage of strictly necessary cookies which serve solely for the transmission of a communication, i.e., for the provision of a service which one has expressly requested, does not require consent. For that reason, our website does not have a consent mechanism (a “cookie banner” with acceptance options).

How to disable cookies through your browser settings?

Although these cookies are necessary for the operation of the website and disabling them may affect the proper submission of the registration form, you may block or delete them at any time through the settings of your internet browser:

Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data

Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and site data

Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Apple Safari: Settings → Privacy → Block all cookies
