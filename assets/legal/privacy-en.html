3. Data Processing

Where a legal person registers its employees or associates for a professional seminar by means of the registration form, INŽENJERSKI BIRO d.o.o. obtains the data concerning those natural persons (first name, surname, business e-mail) not directly from them, but from the legal entity carrying out the registration.

In such cases:

  • The legal entity carrying out the registration shall be obliged to ensure a valid legal basis for the submission of such data to INŽENJERSKI BIRO and to inform its employees/associates that their data will be submitted and processed for the purpose of holding the conference or training program.
  • INŽENJERSKI BIRO d.o.o. shall provide the registered natural persons with information regarding the processing of their personal data by referring them to this Policy.

Purpose of data processing

We process your data solely for necessary and lawful purposes, as follows:

  • Receipt and processing of registrations for professional conferences or training programs
  • Preparation and delivery of quotations
  • Issuing of invoices and pro forma invoices
  • Keeping records of participants and issuing certificates of attendance at professional training in the field of public procurement

  • Delivery of access credentials to participants in conferences that take place online (webinars)
  • Communication with participants - delivery of working materials, certificates of attendance, answers to questions raised and other related information
  • Sending of emails informing of professional conferences or training programs being held
  • Fulfilment of statutory obligations

Legal basis for data processing

  • Taking of steps at the data subject's request prior to entering into a contract, and performance of the contract – preparation of a quotation based on the completed conference or training program registration form and fulfilment of the contractual obligation to hold the conference or training program,

Automated decision-making and profiling

In processing your personal data, we do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Article 22 of the General Data Protection Regulation.

5. Data Retention Period

We store personal data only for as long as is necessary to achieve the purpose for which they were collected, unless the regulations require longer retention.

  • Participants’ contact data – the contact data of conference and training program participants are retained for the duration of the business relationship in order to enable access to the history of conferences and training programs, and for no longer than 5 years from the last business activity with the applicant, after which they are erased or anonymised.
  • Accounting documentation as prescribed by law – documents are retained for 11 years from the end of the business year to which they relate.
  • Enquiries and registrations which do not result in cooperation: retained for a maximum of 2 years from the last communication, based on the legitimate interest in re-establishing business contact, after which they are erased.
  • Strictly necessary cookies: session cookies are deleted from your device immediately upon closing the internet browser; the duration of other cookies is set out in the Cookie Policy.

6. Data Security

The technical and organisational measures which we implement to protect access and to prevent accidental loss, alteration, unauthorised access or misuse of data are the following:

  • Encryption of the connection (SSL/TLS): the website uses a secure HTTPS connection; all data entered in the registration form are encrypted during transmission,
  • Website protection: security monitoring of the server, firewall protection and regular remediation of security vulnerabilities,
  • Restriction of access: access to data from registrations is restricted to authorised personnel in accordance with the need-to-know principle.
